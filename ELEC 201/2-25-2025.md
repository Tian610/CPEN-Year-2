## Diode: ...or an electric valve
Diodes are vacuum tubes with two electrodes. Diodes allow current flow in one direction but blocks it in the other direction.

Diodes let current flow from the black to the white side. Black is designated *A* for **Anode** and white is *C* for **Cathode**. In other words, current moves from **A** to **C**.

$$ V_d = V_A-V_C $$

Unlike normal components, The Voltage Drop across a diode is given by **two** equations:

- If current is flowing in the correct direction ($i_d > 0$), then the voltage drop is $V_d = 0$
- If current is flowing in the incorrect direction ($V_d < 0$), then the current is $i_d = 0$

In other words, in the correct direction it acts as a short circuit, in the incorrect direction it a open circuit.

**If the diode is conducting...**
- We say it is closed
- And it behaves like a wire

**If the diode is NOT conducting...** 
- We say it is open
- And it behaves like an open cirucit


### To solve Circuit Problems...

Do the circuit twice. Once assuming the diode is on and once assuming it is off. Compute $V_A$ and $V_C$ and solve for $V_d$. Then check that $V_d$ matches the expectation of whether the diode is on or off.
